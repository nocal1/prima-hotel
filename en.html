Prima Hotel Antalya

A sea-view retreat in Lara

Located in Lara, Antalya, our hotel offers an enjoyable holiday with sea-view rooms. We welcome guests in 60 rooms in total: 7 suites with kitchen and 53 standard rooms.

Our turn-down service prepares your room for the night, wireless internet keeps you connected in the common areas, and our tour desk helps you book excursions.
